01 About

Beyond the Title

A journey being built across business, technology, finance and digital innovation — one system at a time.

George S. Thomas — known as Thomu — is a Kerala-based entrepreneur and business owner building his journey across business, technology, finance and digital innovation. He is the Owner / Managing Director of NKT Group, a real-world business ecosystem rooted in Thodupuzha, Kerala, and the founder of THOMU LAB, its technology and R&D counterpart.

His work sits at the intersection of things that usually live apart: operating a physical business, understanding the infrastructure behind it, and designing the digital systems that make it measurable. That range — from EV charging operations and property assets to local AI, homelab infrastructure and personal finance architecture — is the point. It is a builder's range, not a specialist's.

He is not trying to look like a traditional software engineer. The role he is growing into is closer to an orchestrator: someone who understands how technology works well enough to connect the right pieces, direct them, and build durable systems around them — across companies, assets and ideas.

Being a young entrepreneur in Kerala — an Indian tech entrepreneur working from a town rather than a metro — has its own advantages. The businesses are physical, the feedback is immediate, and very little can be faked. The work here is documented as it happens: systems tested, assumptions corrected, results left unembellished. The personal brand is a by-product of that building, never the point of it.

“I don't want to simply use technology. I want to understand it, connect it, and build systems around it.”

George S. Thomas

04 Product Vision

Apex Creator OS

Master your cash flow, amplify your wealth.

Envisioned as a personal, family, business and financial operating system — one place where money, goals and entities stop living in scattered spreadsheets and start working as a system. It carries two layers: a financial core and a creator workflow.

One operating system, two layers

Apex Creator OS is designed around a simple idea: a person's financial life is not a pile of accounts — it is a set of entities, each with its own rules. Personal money, family money and business money must be separated before they can be understood. The platform is being architected so that separation is the default, not an afterthought.

On top of that foundation sit the day-to-day systems: budgets and cash flow, investment and net-worth tracking, goals with real consequences, automation for the repetitive work, and a gamified layer that makes consistency feel worth keeping.

Layer 01 · Financial core

Money, entities and goals

Personal, family and business finances separated by default and visible together — cash flow, net worth, investments and Safe-to-Spend, with goals that carry real consequences.

Layer 02 · Creator workflow

Making and publishing

The production side: capture, ingest, edit, grade, publish and archive — so the media that documents the businesses is produced through a repeatable system rather than one-off effort.

06 Finance

Building Wealth,
Not Just Income

Income buys today. Ownership buys tomorrow. The interest is in understanding how capital behaves across markets, assets and time — and in building the systems that keep the process disciplined.

“Investment is responsibility. Trading is choice.”

Investing is treated as a duty to the people and businesses that depend on long-term decisions. Trading is a deliberate, personal pursuit of skill. Keeping the two separate is a discipline, not a detail: capital meant for a decade should never be run like capital meant for a week.

Approach first, instruments second. Decisions are made through research, position sizing, risk tolerance and time horizon — never through noise, hype or borrowed conviction.

The objective is not income for its own sake, but wealth building toward financial freedom: the point at which decisions stop being made out of need and start being made out of intent.

09 NKT Charge Hub

From Infrastructure to Intelligent Mobility

NKT Charge Hub is an EV charging station in Thodupuzha, Kerala — and operating an EV charging business changes how you think about technology. A charging point is not a plug; it is an energy system, a software system and a service business at the same time.

Charging infrastructure

DC fast charging with CCS2 connectors — physical infrastructure planned and maintained for real usage, not for appearance.

Charging management

OCPP-based management is the direction: standards-based control instead of proprietary lock-in, so the site can evolve with the network.

Energy systems

Understanding load, demand and uptime — the electrical reality behind every charging session.

Smart charging & automation

Where the charging business meets THOMU LAB: scheduling, monitoring and automated reporting as the systems mature.

Digital monitoring & security

Site visibility through digital monitoring and CCTV — operational awareness as a baseline, not a luxury.

The wider EV ecosystem

Charging is the entry point. Vehicle technology, energy storage and intelligent mobility are the long-horizon interests around it.
