Privacy Policy

This website is built to collect as little as possible. The short version: no advertising trackers, no contact form, and no third-party requests on page load — and analytics stays switched off unless you choose to turn it on.

Plain English, no filler

What this website collects

Nothing is collected by this website itself. There is no login, no sign-up, no newsletter, no comment system and no contact form — so there is no form for you to type personal data into.

  • Analytics is off until you turn it on. Google Analytics 4 is wired up but silent: nothing is requested from Google, and no analytics cookie is set, until you press Accept in the consent card. No tag manager, no advertising or social pixels.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, icons and images are served from this domain. No content is loaded from Google Fonts, CDNs or external image hosts.
  • No cookies by default. The site itself sets none. If you accept analytics, Google sets its own _ga cookies — and only from that moment.

The two things stored on your device

Two preferences are saved in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers them on your next visit: your Reduce motion choice (thomu:motion) and, if you have answered it, your analytics choice (thomu:analytics). Neither contains personal information and neither leaves your device. Clearing your browser storage removes both.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics here is opt-in, and it works like this:

  • Google Consent Mode v2 starts in the denied state — nothing is stored or sent before you decide.
  • A small card asks once; Decline keeps the site completely tracker-free, and your answer is remembered on this device.
  • Nothing is loaded from Google at all until you press Accept. Until then the site makes zero third-party requests.

You can revisit the choice — or confirm that no trackers are in use — through Cookie settings in the footer of any page. Dismissing the card without choosing counts as a decline.

Server logs

Like almost every website, this one is served by a host that may record standard technical request information (such as IP address, user agent and requested URL) for security, reliability and abuse prevention. Those logs are controlled by the hosting provider, not by this site, and are not used here to build profiles of visitors.

External links

Links to LinkedIn and Instagram leave this website. Once you are on those platforms, their own privacy policies apply — this site has no control over them and receives nothing from them.

Email

If you email gdrivegeorge@gmail.com, your message is received in a normal mailbox. It is used to answer you and, where relevant, to continue a business or technical conversation. It is not sold, published, added to a marketing list, or used for anything other than the correspondence you started.

Never send sensitive information by email to this address: passwords, OTPs, PINs, card numbers, CVV codes, bank statements, tax documents, private contracts, API keys or credentials. If something sensitive arrives in error, write back asking for it to be deleted.

What this website will never publish

The public layer of this site is deliberately narrow. The following never appear anywhere on this domain:

  • Bank account numbers, card numbers, CVV, PIN or OTP
  • Balances, statements, tax documents, portfolio holdings or private financial figures
  • Passwords, API keys, server, CCTV or backend credentials
  • Private home addresses, private phone numbers and private family information
  • Private business contracts, internal monitoring data and operational secrets

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by this website, there is usually nothing to access, correct or erase. If you believe any information about you has been published here in error, email gdrivegeorge@gmail.com and it will be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

Changes

If this site ever adds a contact form, an embedded service or any other data-processing feature, this page will be updated before that change goes live. It was last updated when consent-gated analytics was switched on.
