02 Gaming

Performance as a hobby, not a personality

PC gaming, gaming hardware and the performance question underneath it. It is a genuine interest, a release valve, and an honest route into understanding hardware — GPUs, thermals, latency and the trade-offs that make a system fast.

Platform

PC gaming

Strategy, simulation and competitive play on custom-built machines — where the frame rate is a systems problem worth solving.

Hardware

Custom PCs & GPUs

Building and tuning: CPU and GPU choices, cooling, storage, display and the quiet satisfaction of a stable overclock.

Desk

Peripherals & setup

Keyboards, mice, audio and displays judged on how much friction they remove over a long session.

Curiosity

Game development

An interest in how games are built — engines, rendering and simulation — kept as an interest rather than presented as a skill.

Presented as a premium hobby: no streaming stats, no rankings, no claimed achievements — only the hardware and the curiosity.

03 3D Printing

From CAD to a part you can hold

Rapid prototyping is the shortest route from an idea to something testable. 3D printing is where design, patience and material behaviour meet — and where a failed print still teaches you something.

  • 3D printing
  • CAD
  • Prototyping
  • Rapid prototyping
  • Custom parts
  • Hardware experiments
  • Maker projects
  • Enclosures & mounts

Most prints are practical: brackets, mounts, organisers, jigs and replacements for parts that nobody sells. Occasionally one becomes the answer to a question a business asked.
