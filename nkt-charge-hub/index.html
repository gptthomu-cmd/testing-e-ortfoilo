02 Operations

A charging point is three businesses at once

An energy system, a software system and a service business — operating one changes how you think about technology, uptime and infrastructure.

Hardware

Charging infrastructure

90 kW DC fast charging delivered through dual-gun CCS2 connectors — physical infrastructure specified, installed and maintained for real usage rather than for appearance.

Software

Charging management

OCPP-compatible charging infrastructure means standards-based control instead of proprietary lock-in, so the site can evolve with the network around it.

Energy

Load, demand and uptime

Sanctioned load, demand patterns and availability are the electrical reality behind every session — the part of EV infrastructure that marketing never mentions.

Automation

Monitoring & reporting

Where the charging business meets THOMU LAB: digital monitoring, automated reporting and alerting as the systems mature.

Security

Site visibility

Digital monitoring and CCTV give operational awareness as a baseline. Credentials and access details are private and stay that way.

Long view

The wider EV ecosystem

Charging is the entry point. Vehicle technology, energy storage and intelligent mobility are the long-horizon interests built around it.
