The short version

George S. Thomas — known publicly as Thomu, and online as thomudidnot — is a Kerala-based entrepreneur building across business, technology, finance and digital innovation. He runs NKT Group, a real-world business ecosystem rooted in Thodupuzha, and THOMU LAB, its technology and R&D counterpart.

His work sits at the intersection of things that usually live apart: operating a physical business, understanding the infrastructure behind it, and designing the digital systems that make it measurable. That range — from EV charging operations and property assets to local AI, homelab infrastructure and personal finance architecture — is the point. It is a builder's range, not a specialist's, and the honest description of it is jack of all trades, master of none: the value is in the connections, not in any single depth.

He is not trying to look like a traditional software engineer. The role being grown into is closer to an orchestrator: someone who understands how technology works well enough to connect the right pieces, direct them, and build durable systems around them — across companies, assets and ideas. A vibe coder in the useful sense: AI-assisted, shipping-oriented, and happy to be judged on what runs.

Being a young entrepreneur in Kerala — an Indian technology entrepreneur working from a town rather than a metro — has its own advantages. The businesses are physical, the feedback is immediate, and very little can be faked. Systems get tested, assumptions get corrected, and results stay unembellished.

How the work gets judged

  • Progress = shipped products. A system that runs beats a plan that sounds good.
  • Build systems, not just ideas. An idea is a starting point, not an asset.
  • Money works for me. Income is a step; ownership and cash flow are the goal.
  • Dream in mind → work in silence → build with purpose → leave a legacy.

03 Boundaries

Public layer, private layer

This website runs on a strict two-layer model. There is a lot happening behind it; what appears here is everything that has been deliberately chosen for public view.

Public layer — what you will find

  • Identity: George S. Thomas, Thomu, thomudidnot
  • Business: NKT Group, NKT Charge Hub, Nedumpurath Towers, NKT Vessels House
  • Technology: THOMU LAB, Apex Creator OS, My_AI_OS
  • Interests: AI, automation, open source, gaming, 3D printing, photography, EV technology, homelab
  • Social: LinkedIn, Instagram
  • Contact: one public portfolio email

Private layer — what never appears

  • Bank accounts, card numbers, CVV, PIN or OTP
  • Balances, statements, tax documents, private holdings
  • Passwords, API keys, server or CCTV credentials
  • Private addresses, private contracts, private family documents
  • Any figure that is not intentionally and explicitly published

Show the identity. Show the ambition. Show the work. Keep the sensitive details private — that is the rule this site is built on, and it is the reason nothing here is padded out with numbers that cannot be stood behind.
